Good to know

  • Plants

    In autumn, water less as the nights get colder. Bring in any plant that can't handle frost before nights drop close to 0 °C.

  • Cats

    Lilies are very poisonous to cats, even the pollen. Check that a new plant is cat-safe before it goes on the balcony.

  • Home

    Use unscented cleaners around the litter boxes, because strong smells can put cats off using them. Never mix bleach with ammonia.
